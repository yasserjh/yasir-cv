ABOUT

Delivery first. Technology where it helps.

I came into project delivery through engineering. Working around live operations taught me that strong delivery depends on clear ownership, disciplined interfaces, readiness, and controls that people can actually use.

When repetitive workflows or fragmented data get in the way, I build practical tools around the work—automation, reporting, and decision-support systems that reduce manual handling without replacing the governance behind the process. My MiM at MBSC adds a business and management layer to that technical and delivery foundation.

SELECTED WORK

Digital tools built from real delivery and operational problems.

These are not standalone software experiments. Each started with a recurring workflow or analysis problem I encountered in delivery and operations. Public visuals use fictional data and generic naming.

Workflow Automation

Operations Document Automation Suite

Challenge: A high-volume work-notification process required repeated preparation, validation, planning checks, and controlled outputs.

Built: A C#/.NET desktop application that structures work details, validates inputs, combines infrastructure impacts, tracks planning attention, and supports controlled document output.

Value: Moved a fragmented sequence of manual checks into one governed workflow, reducing repeated handling and making the process easier to review and repeat.

Operational Intelligence

Operations Intelligence Platform

Challenge: Large operational logs made recurring patterns and specific historical events slow to retrieve and compare.

Built: A read-only C#/.NET analytics workspace with synchronized data, structured search, filters, trend views, incident exploration, and reporting.

Value: Made historical records searchable and comparable without changing the source data, so reviews can start from evidence instead of manual log hunting.
